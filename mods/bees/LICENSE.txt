models/bees_bee.obj, textures/bees_bee_*.png and sounds/bees_bee.1-7.ogg are the bee model and textures from the "X Farming" mod by SaKeL
(https://content.luanti.org/packages/SaKeL/x_farming/), renamed only (x_farming_bee.N.ogg -> bees_bee.N.ogg). Media license: CC-BY-SA-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Shared under the same license.
The author states the project is entirely handmade. If that is ever shown to be wrong, replace these four files (they are referenced in one place, init.lua).
bees_bee_features_angry.png is our recolour (red eyes) of bees_bee_features.png, made by tools/make_bee_angry_eyes.py; same license.

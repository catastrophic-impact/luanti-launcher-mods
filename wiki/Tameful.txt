================================================================
TAMEFUL
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, uikit (included)
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         tameful

Tame (almost) any animal with a lead. Pets follow you, stay, relax at home, fight
for you, carry you, and never die for good: a pet that is downed goes to your
stable and comes back when it has recovered.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Taming
- Right-click an animal with a lead and choose Yes in the popup. The lead is
  used up (setting).
- Animals you can already tame the usual way (wolves, cats, horses and so on)
  join your pet list too.
- Baby animals can't be tamed. Wait until they have grown up.
- If you already have as many pets out as the max_summoned setting allows
  (default 1), a newly tamed pet goes straight to the stable.
- Skeleton horses and zombie horses can be tamed.
- Not tameable: water creatures that cannot live on land.
- A pet keeps its name tag blocked: rename it in the pet window instead.

Recipes
- 3 string in a diagonal = 1 lead.
- Saddles can be made from rabbit hide or wool instead of leather, and with a
  copper ingot instead of iron (same shape as the normal saddle).

Controls
- Right-click your pet with an empty hand: Follow / Stay. On animals you get on
  when you right-click (horses and so on) hold sneak while right-clicking.
- Right-click your pet with a stick: opens its pet window.
- /tameful opens your pet list.

Pet window
- Picture, kind, health, mode and saddle.
- Stats: speed, jump, attack and the sword it counts as, armor and its points,
  crit chance.
- Rename, Follow / Stay / Relax, Attitude, Wander and Range buttons, Ride,
  Stable and Release.
- Animals with an inventory (horses, donkeys, mules, llamas) get an Inventory
  button.

Stable and recovery
- Stable puts a pet away; Resummon calls it back next to you.
- A pet that would die is downed instead: it goes to the stable at full health
  and can be called back after downed_seconds (default 30).
- Pets that follow you are stabled when you log out or change dimension.
  Pets that stay or relax are left where they are.
- Release sets a pet free and gives its saddle and horse armor back to you.

Modes and fighting
- Follow: the pet walks with you. Every pet walks at the same speed (3.5
  blocks/s wandering, about 7 following), whatever the animal.
- Stay: the pet stays where it is.
- Relax: the pet stays near a home spot and wanders (Wander 6 / 12 / 24 blocks).
- Attitude: Passive never fights. Defensive fights whoever hurt it, you or
  what you hit. Aggressive also attacks hostile mobs near you (never creepers)
  within its Range (default 16). Pets never hunt other animals on their own.
- Pets have their own health, attack and armor per species, and heal 1 health
  every 2 seconds after 10 seconds without damage (setting).
- Babies ignore pet parents.

Riding
- Ride from the pet window. Land pets can be ridden: the walking speed and
  jump come from the species, a saddle adds 2 blocks/s on the animals that
  take one (horse family, pig, strider).
- Pets climb one-block steps while ridden. Sneak gets you off. You can ride
  through water.
- Flying pets (bats, parrots) can't be ridden yet.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
/tameful          Open your pet list and the settings. (Everyone; settings only
                  for the world owner or players with the server privilege.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Change them in the /tameful screen. Saved in the mod's storage in the world.

consume_lead      on      The lead is used up when an animal is tamed.
max_tames         10      Most pets per player (0 = no limit).
max_summoned      1       Most pets out in the world at once (0 = no limit).
downed_seconds    30      How long a downed pet recovers in the stable.
immortal          on      Pets are downed instead of dying for good.
follow_teleport   24      A following pet farther away than this is brought to you.
show_name         on      Write the pet's name above it.
show_health       off     Also show its health on its own line.
show_owner        off     Also show its owner on its own line.
regen_health      on      Pets heal over time.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed, a "Tameful" button in the HQ list runs /tameful.
Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Works with Mob Crate: a crated pet can still be stabled, and a stale copy
  is removed if you release it after that.
- Works with Conflict Resolution: other players can't hurt your pets by
  default.
- Pet data is saved with the animal and in the world, so it survives restarts.

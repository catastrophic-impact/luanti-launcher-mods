================================================================
NO SLEEP
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, uikit (included)
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         nosleep

Beds set your respawn point but you can never sleep: the night has to be
survived.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Right-click a bed at any time of day to set your respawn point. You don't
  lie down and the night doesn't pass.
- The usual space rules for a bed still apply.
- "Respawn point set" shows in the action bar.
- Beds in the Nether and the End still explode, as normal.
- The world owner can allow sleeping again, so beds work as usual.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
/nosleep          Open the settings. (Everyone can look; only the world owner or
                  players with the server privilege can change them.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Saved in the mod's storage in the world.

allow_sleeping    off    Allow sleeping again (beds work as normal).

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed, a "No Sleep" button in the HQ list runs /nosleep.
Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Respawn anchors also show their message in the action bar.

================================================================
DUNGEONEER
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, multiversatile (included), uikit (included)
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         dungeoneer

Random dungeons that are built when you ask for one, in a dimension of their own.
Every run is different, every monster is placed by hand (nothing spawns on its own),
and a boss waits at the far end. A dungeon is taken away again when nobody has been
inside for a while. You enter through a small portal that you light with a key
(operators can also use a command).

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Dungeons
- A castle dungeon: a grid of 6 x 5 rooms joined by corridors (every room can be
  reached), a start room on the west side and the boss room at the far east end.
  Rooms differ in size and every run has a different layout.
- Each run lives in its own area of the Dungeon dimension. Up to 10 dungeons can
  exist at once; when one is removed its area is cleaned completely and used again.
- Dark and closed in: no sky, no sun, no moon, no stars, no clouds, no weather.
- Tiers 1 to 4: higher tiers mean tougher monsters (they take less damage) and a
  tougher boss.

The portal and keys
- Dungeon Portal Frame (craft 8: stone bricks in the four corners and obsidian in the four edges of the crafting grid, centre empty;
  mined with a diamond pickaxe). Build a 4x4 ring of them standing up, in either
  direction, around a 2x2 opening; the four corners are not needed.
- Dungeon Keys, one per tier. They cannot be crafted:
  - Tier I drops (1%) from any hostile mob that a player kills, in any dimension. Mobs
    inside dungeons never drop keys.
  - Tier II is in the chest of a tier I dungeon, tier III in the chest of a tier II
    dungeon, tier IV in the chest of a tier III dungeon. A tier IV chest holds no key.
  - When the boss dies, every player who is inside at that moment (a player who has
    died but not yet respawned still counts) is owed one key of the next tier. The
    chest holds one per player; only they can take theirs, and only one each, even if
    they come back for it later through the portal.
  - You can carry only one key, of any tier. A mob drop is skipped, a key on the
    ground stays there and the chest will not give you a key while you carry one.
  Use a key on the frame: a new dungeon of that tier is built, the opening lights up
  (teal) and the key is used up (in creative mode too). A key is lost if the run is
  lost: you start again from a tier I drop.
- Every lit portal has its own dungeon.
- Step into the portal and a window asks what to do:
  - Enter: you arrive at the start of that run. Everyone who enters through the
    portal goes to the same run.
  - Reset: removes the dungeon and the portal after one more question (it warns if
    a chest or grave with items was left inside). Greyed out while anybody is
    inside. The frame stays: light it again with a key.
  - Cancel: nothing happens, and it does not ask again until you step out and back in.
  The window shows the tier, who is inside, how long until an empty dungeon closes,
  and whether the boss has been defeated.
- A key used on a lit portal (or its frame) while nobody is inside replaces that
  dungeon with a new one of the key's tier. With somebody inside it does nothing and
  you keep the key.
- The way out: a portal in the start room and another in the boss room. Stepping
  into one asks Leave or Stay (it shows whether the boss is dead, who else stays
  inside, and how long the dungeon lasts after you leave). Leave takes you back to
  just outside the portal you came in by (a block or so away, facing away). Stay
  does nothing, and it does not ask again until you step out and back in.
- Log in while standing in the Dungeon dimension (you logged out inside, the server
  restarted, the dungeon was removed while you were away) and you are put outside:
  at the place you came in from, or your spawn.
- No new dungeons from inside a dungeon: a key does nothing in the Dungeon dimension.
- When an empty dungeon's time runs out its portal goes out (the frame stays) and
  the dungeon is removed. Break a frame block and the portal closes: anybody inside
  stays and walks out by the exit portals, and the dungeon is removed as soon as it
  is empty (at once if nobody is inside).

Unbreakable walls
- The walls, floor and lanterns are Dungeon Bricks, Dungeon Floor and Dungeon
  Lantern: unbreakable like bedrock (only creative mode can break them) and immune
  to explosions and pistons. Anything you place yourself in a dungeon is an
  ordinary block and can be broken as usual.

Monsters
- Packs of monsters wait in the rooms (zombies and skeletons; from tier 2 husks and
  spiders; from tier 3 strays and vindicators; tier 4 adds wither skeletons).
  Nothing else ever spawns. They never go away for being far from players.

The boss
- One boss at the end: a zombie twice the normal size, with a random name, a boss
  bar, lots of health and armor.
- Abilities grow with the tier: it calls zombie helpers (tier 1, at most 4 alive),
  makes a telegraphed ground slam, a ring of flames shows where it lands (tier 2),
  and teleports to players who keep their distance (tier 3). Tier 4 does all of it,
  faster.
- It notices anybody within 24 blocks, even through walls.
- Beating the boss marks the dungeon as cleared.

Loot
- A chest waits in a corner of the boss room, filled from the dungeon's tier (better
  loot at higher tiers). It is sealed until the boss is dead: it will not open and
  cannot be broken. Beating the boss tells you the chest has unsealed.

No friendly fire
- Dungeon monsters never hurt each other (arrows, bites, summoned helpers). They
  still hurt you, your pets and anything else, and you can hurt them.

Resets
- A dungeon is removed after 10 minutes with nobody inside (a setting). Everything in
  it is removed: blocks, monsters, and whatever you left behind, and its portal
  goes out.
- Optional: remove it as soon as it is empty after a player died in it.
- A chest, grave or other block with items left in a dungeon keeps it alive a
  while longer (60 minutes extra by default).
- Monsters left behind in saved map blocks of a removed dungeon are removed when
  they load.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS (operators)
----------------------------------------------------------------
/dungeon spawn castle <1-4>    build a new dungeon of that tier and enter it
/dungeon list                  the dungeons that exist
/dungeon leave                 go back to where you came from
/dungeon reset <id>            take a dungeon away (refused while somebody is inside)
/dungeon setting reset_minutes <1-1440>
/dungeon setting reset_on_death <true|false>
/dungeon setting grave_grace_minutes <0-10080>
/dungeon settings              open the settings screen

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Per world, kept in the mod storage of the world. Open the screen with
/dungeon settings, or with the Dungeoneer button in HQ (only operators, and the owner
of a single-player world, can change them):

reset_minutes         10     How long an empty dungeon lives.
reset_on_death        off    Remove it as soon as it is empty after a player died.
grave_grace_minutes   60     Extra time for a dungeon that still holds a chest or grave.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed, a "Dungeoneer" button in the HQ list opens the settings screen.
Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Players who join a dungeon don't need anything but the mod.
- Peaceful difficulty removes hostile monsters, including the dungeon's.
- Keys do nothing inside the dungeon dimension (no portals inside portals).
- The dungeon is a band of heights in the one Luanti map, below the Overworld
  (see Multiversatile); it is not a separate world file.
- Dungeon monsters are Mineclonia's own mobs.

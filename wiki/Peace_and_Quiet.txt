================================================================
PEACE AND QUIET
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, uikit (included)
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         peaceandquiet

Hides the advancement popups so nothing jumps onto your screen. It is a
per-player switch and the popups are hidden by default.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Advancement popups and their sound are hidden. You still earn the
  advancement, its prizes and its experience.
- The advancement line in chat stays.
- Each player chooses for themselves. Hidden by default.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
/peaceandquiet    Open the settings. (Everyone.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Saved on the player.

hide_advancements   on    Hide advancement popups.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed, a "Peace and Quiet" button in the HQ list runs
/peaceandquiet. Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Recipe, tutorial and system popups don't exist in Mineclonia, so there is
  nothing else to hide.

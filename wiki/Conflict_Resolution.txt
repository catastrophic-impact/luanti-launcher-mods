================================================================
CONFLICT RESOLUTION
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, uikit (included)
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         conflictresolution

Keep the peace on a shared world: turn player-versus-player damage on or off and
protect tamed animals from other players.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Rules
- PvP: when off, players can't hurt each other. Off by default.
- Protect pets: other players can't hurt your tamed animals. On by default.
- Protect own pets: you can't hurt your own tamed animals either. On by default.

What counts as a player's attack
- Punches and weapons, arrows and tridents the player shot, hits by a tamed
  animal (counted as its owner), and TNT the player lit.
- Chain-reaction TNT is not attributed to anyone.
- Hits with no player behind them (lava, fire, wild mobs) are never blocked.

Feedback
- A blocked attacker sees a short message in the action bar.
- Changes are announced in chat.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
/pvp                       Show the current settings. (Everyone.)
/pvp on | off              Turn PvP on or off. (World owner or players with the server privilege.)
/pvp pets on | off         Protect other players' pets on or off. (Same.)
/pvp ownpets on | off      Protect your own pets from you on or off. (Same.)
/cr                        Open the settings screen. (Everyone can look; only the
                           owner or server privilege can change.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Saved in the mod's storage in the world.

pvp             off     Players can hurt each other.
protect_pets    on      Other players' pets are protected.
protect_own     on      Owners can't hurt their own pets.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed, a "Conflict Resolution" button in the HQ list runs /cr.
Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Works with Tameful pets and any tamed animal that has an owner.

================================================================
POPULATIONS
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, uikit (included)
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         populations

Villages full of people: villagers become men and women with names, player skins
and families, and they stand up for each other.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
People
- Every villager is a man or a woman with a player skin: Steve-style for men,
  Alex-style (slim arms) for women. Zombie villagers look like ordinary zombies.
- Villages stay close to half men, half women, including newborns and spawn
  eggs.
- Every villager gets a random first and last name, also used in death
  messages and the trade window title. A name tag still renames them.
- Name tags show name, job and health, up to 32 blocks away.
- Children are half size.
- No more villager "hmm" sounds.

Families
- Only a man and a woman can have a baby.
- Their first baby makes them partners: she takes his last name, and so does
  the baby.
- Partners only have children together while both are alive.
- If one partner dies, the other may start a new family.
- A zombified and cured villager is still the same person.

Gathering
- At most 6 villagers walk to a bell at the same time; the others pace around the
  village. Villagers also push each other apart a little so they do not stand
  inside one another.

Health and fighting
- Adults have 40 health, children 20. Everyone heals 1 health every 2 seconds
  after 10 seconds out of combat.
- Adults fight back against mobs and players that hurt them, in armor and with
  a sword (leather and stone by default). The gear is gone after the fight.
- Each hit a villager takes has a chance to call one helper (10% on the first
  hit, 20% on the second, up to 60%).
- At half health a villager stops fighting and runs. Every hit it takes while
  running calls one more helper for sure.
- Villagers calm down 10 seconds after the fighting stops.
- Children never fight (they always run).

Iron golems
- 30% faster, 25% stronger, and spot enemies from 32 blocks (can be turned
  off).

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
/pop    Open the settings screen. (World owner or players with the server privilege.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Change them with /pop. Saved in the mod's storage in the world.

stronger_golems              on       Faster, stronger golems with longer sight.
villagers_defend_themselves  on       Villagers fight whoever hurts them.
villagers_can_aid_others     on       Villagers come to help each other.
villager_armor               leather  none, leather, chain, copper, iron, gold,
                                      diamond, netherite
villager_sword               stone    wood, stone, copper, iron, gold, diamond,
                                      netherite

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed, a "Populations" button in the HQ list opens /pop.
Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Changes the existing villagers. It adds no new ones, so villages, trading and
  jobs work as normal.
- Each person is saved with the villager, so names and families survive
  restarts.

================================================================
IDENTIFIABLE
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, uikit (included)
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         identifiable

A small panel names the block, creature, player or item you look at, with health,
harvest info, crop growth, light level and more.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Panel
- Name, picture and id of what you are looking at (through water too).
- Creatures: health and armor. Players: name, optionally health.
- Blocks: harvest line (which tool and level), crop growth, villager job and
  tier, the mob inside a spawner, light level (several modes) and whether
  mobs could spawn there.
- Breaking bar: a bar at the bottom of the panel shows how far the block you
  are digging has come (survival).
- Long lines wrap to half the window width, up to 8 lines.
- Five backgrounds: Dark, Button, Advancement, Tooltip, None. Nine text colors
  and four text sizes.

Zoom
- Hold the zoom key (Z) to zoom. The panel reaches farther while zoomed.

Position
- Place the panel anywhere: Position mode in /idconfig, then click where it
  should go, or align left, center or right.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
/idconfig         Open your settings with a live preview. (Everyone.)
/idhide           Hide or show the panel.
/idblacklist      Hide (or show again) the thing you are looking at.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Per player, in /idconfig. Saved on the player. Reset restores the defaults but
keeps your hidden list.

enabled, show_id, show_mod_name, show_icon       on
show_health, show_armor, show_block_details      on
show_harvest, show_villager, show_items          on
show_spawner, show_breaking                      on
show_player_health, show_spawnable               off
light_mode          off     off, light, sky, both, combined
range               5       5, 10, 16 or 32 blocks
text_align          left    left, center, right
text_size           normal  small, normal, large, huge
hud_color           white   nine colors
background_style    dark    dark, button, advancement, tooltip, none

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed there are three buttons: "Identifiable" opens /idconfig,
"hide/show panel" runs /idhide, "hide what I look at" runs /idblacklist.
Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- The panel is drawn by the server, so it needs no client mod.
- Text width is estimated, so very long names may wrap slightly early or late.

================================================================
WAVE MATRIX
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, uikit (included); optional wm_music for your own songs
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         wavematrix

Your own music player: twelve bundled tracks, your own .ogg songs, favorites and
the Mineclonia music discs, all playing for you alone.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Playing
- Music plays for each player on their own, with their own settings. Nothing is
  downloaded when you join: a song is sent to you the first time you play it.
- Mode: Shuffle, In order or Loop.
- Immersion: Natural (a random pause of about 3 to 8 minutes between songs) or
  Continuous (song after song).
- Plays: All songs or only your Favorites.
- Volume. Changing it restarts the current song at the same spot.
- Show song name: the song's name appears on screen while it plays.
- A song starts about 5 seconds after you join, unless you pressed Stop.

The window
- A list of songs in groups, a Now playing area with a progress bar, Play,
  Stop and Next, and a star to mark favorites.
- Click a group's title to put the whole group in or out of Shuffle and In
  order. Click a song to select it; the note button plays it.
- The window updates while a song plays.

Songs
- Bundled: twelve tracks, shown as "Track 1" to "Track 12".
- Your own: put .ogg files in the music folder. Every subfolder becomes a group.
- Music discs: the Mineclonia discs are a group of their own, left out by
  default.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
/wm               Open the Wave Matrix window. (Everyone.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Per player, in the /wm window. Saved on the player.

mode              Shuffle      Shuffle, In order or Loop.
immersion         Natural      Natural or Continuous.
plays             All          All songs or Favorites.
volume            50%          Playback volume.
show_name         on           Show the song name while playing.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed, a "Wave Matrix" button in the HQ list runs /wm.
Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Only .ogg songs are supported.
- Your own songs live in a shared music folder (the wm_music mod, turned on
  for each world that uses Wave Matrix). On a server it is the server's folder,
  so only people with access to it can add songs.
- The launcher's Folders tab manages the music folder.
- The bundled tracks are all rights reserved and for use with this mod only.

================================================================
MOB CRATE
================================================================
Version:        1.0.0
Requires:       Mineclonia, uikit (included)
Install on:     Server (works in single player too)
Mod ID:         mobcrate

Pack up a mob, carry it in your inventory, let it out somewhere else: the same
mob comes back, with its health, name and everything else.

----------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Craft a Mob Crate with 9 wood planks (any kind). It stacks to 16.
- Right-click or left-click a mob with an empty crate to pack it. You get a
  Full Mob Crate (one per stack) named after the mob.
- Right-click a block with a full crate to let the mob out. It faces you.
  You get the empty crate back.
- Not allowed: players, items and vehicles, bosses (setting), another player's
  tamed animal (setting), dead mobs, mobs riding or carrying another mob, mobs
  on your blacklist, and anything inside a protected area.
- A player riding the mob is lifted off first.
- If there is no room for the mob, the crate stays full and nothing is lost.
  If your inventory is full the crate drops at your feet.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMMANDS
----------------------------------------------------------------
/mobcrate         Open the settings. (World owner or players with the server privilege.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Change them with /mobcrate. Saved in the mod's storage in the world.

allow_bosses              off    Bosses can be put in a crate.
allow_other_players_pets  off    Other players' tamed animals can be put in a crate.
blacklist                 empty  Mob ids that never go in a crate, separated by
                                 commas, for example mobs_mc:villager.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HQ COMPATIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------
With HQ installed, a "Mob Crate" button in the HQ list runs /mobcrate.
Without HQ the mod works exactly the same.

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY & NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Works with Tameful: a crated pet can still be stabled.
- Works with Populations: the person's name and family come back with them.
- A crate lost in lava or the void takes the mob with it.
